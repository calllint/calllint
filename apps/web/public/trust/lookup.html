    A CallLint Trust Page publishes an observation at a specific artifact digest and time under stated completeness. It is not a certification, an endorsement, or a guarantee of safety, and it reports four independent dimensions separately rather than a single score. A page a maintainer has claimed also carries a Verified Publisher note — that records who controls the namespace, never that the tool is safe, and it does not change the observed verdict. Identity verification does not change the CallLint verdict. Something look wrong on a page? Report a correction.
